> HSP presents **A HitchHiker's Guide to GPT**, an interactive hands on workshop on building a lightweight GPT model from scratch right in your browser.

Join us for a practical session where you will explore the foundations of modern generative language models and build your own mini GPT model step by step.

<br>

<h3 style="font-style: italic;"><em>What's in Store</em></h3>

* Build your own text generating model from raw text, constructing the full pipeline from tokenization and embeddings to self attention and text generation.
* Unpack the intuition and key mechanisms behind Generative Pretrained Transformers.
* Run everything 100% in browser using Google Colab with no local GPU setup or fancy hardware needed.
* Get a beginner friendly introduction to Machine Learning with no prior ML background required, just basic python and a ton of curiosity!
* Write and explore the code line by line in a hands on session, leaving with your own working project.

By the end of the session, you will have built your own mini GPT model and developed a clear understanding of how it operates under the hood.
